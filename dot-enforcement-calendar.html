International Roadcheck

May 12–14, 2026 · 72 hours

The big one — a 72-hour, all-hands blitz of full Level I inspections across North America.

✅ Every truck road-ready: brakes, tires, lights, load secure, plus driver docs & HOS current.

🚦

Operation Safe Driver Week

July 12–18, 2026 · 7 days

Targets unsafe driver behavior — speeding, seatbelts, phone use, following too close, reckless driving.

✅ Remind drivers: slow down, buckle up, phone down. These violations stick to the CDL.

🛑

Brake Safety Week

Aug 23–29, 2026 · 7 days

Brake systems & components. Out-of-adjustment or defective brakes = out of service on the spot.

✅ Confirm every truck's brake inspection is current before you dispatch it.

⚠️

Brake Safety Day

Unannounced
One surprise day — sometime in 2026

A one-day brake enforcement push that CVSA does not announce in advance.

✅ Keep brakes compliant year-round — this one can land on any day.

Every year, same rhythm: Roadcheck ~mid-May, Safe Driver Week ~mid-July, Brake Safety Week ~late August, plus an unannounced Brake Safety Day. Dates shown are the official 2026 dates; CVSA sets each year's exact dates — ask your admin to refresh this when the 2027 schedule is posted.
