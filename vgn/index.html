FAQ

Questions drivers ask

Is orientation travel covered?
Yes. The company covers your travel to orientation.
Is this a W-2 company driver job?
No. This is a 1099 independent-contractor rental/lease opportunity. You run your truck as a business, with our dispatch, maintenance and after-hours support behind you.
When do I get paid?
Every Monday, by direct deposit. Your split is 82% on a rental truck or 84% on a lease truck.
What happens if my truck breaks down?
Breakdown protection means no truck payment while the truck is in the shop. If you're stranded, emergency hotels, flights and transportation are covered.
Do you run a credit check?
No. There is no credit check.
What are the driver requirements?
A valid CDL-A with 2 years minimum, OTR experience, no DUI in the last 2 years and no SAP history.
Where do you run?
The Midwest, South, Southeast, Mid-Atlantic and Northeast. We don't run the Mountain states, the West Coast or New York City.
What does the 18% fee cover?
Dispatch, after-hours support, maintenance and permits, except OR & CT permits. Tolls are the driver's responsibility.
