Let’s talk
Who is this opportunity for?

Experienced CDL-A drivers considering an independent-contractor rental, lease-purchase or owner-operator arrangement. Valid Class A CDL, minimum 2 years’ experience and no DUI. Completed all stages of SAP? You’re welcome to apply. Up to 1 preventable accident considered; maximum 1 moving violation in the last 12 months.

Can I get home weekly?

Weekly home time is possible depending on where you live. Agree on dates with dispatch and discuss how your time at home affects the week’s earning opportunities.

What happens if my truck goes into the shop?

Your truck note stops while the truck is in the shop under the applicable program terms. Breakdown hotels and transportation are company-paid. Review the exact coverage and any exclusions for your chosen program before you sign.

What costs should I expect each week?

Compare the program charges above with your actual truck and insurance quote. Fuel, tolls, DEF and other variable costs may apply. Lease-purchase truck payment, truck physical damage and cargo coverage need a current quote. Review a complete settlement with us before you commit.

How does escrow work?

The stated escrow balance is $3,500 and may be lower depending on your driver record. Ask about the funding schedule, permitted uses and return conditions. It is a separate balance, not a quoted weekly fee.

How do I get to orientation?

If you’re approved, VGN books and pays for orientation travel. Ray or Carl will explain arrival arrangements and next steps.
