Who is this opportunity for?

Experienced CDL-A drivers considering an independent-contractor rental, lease-purchase or owner-operator arrangement—not a W-2 company-driver position. Minimum 2 years’ experience; no DUI. Completed all stages of SAP? You’re welcome to apply. Up to 1 preventable accident considered; maximum 1 moving violation in the last 12 months.

Can I get home weekly?

Weekly home time is possible depending on your location. Agree on dates with dispatch and discuss how your time at home changes the week’s earning opportunities.

What happens when my truck goes into the shop?

Your truck note stops while the truck is in the shop under the applicable program terms. Breakdown hotels and transportation are company-paid. Review the exact coverage and any exclusions for your chosen program before you sign.

How does escrow work?

The stated escrow balance is $3,500 and may be lower depending on your driver record. Its funding schedule, permitted uses and return terms should be explained by recruiting before you commit.

What is included in the weekly costs?

The program cards list the weekly charges. Fuel, tolls, DEF and other variable charges may apply. Lease-purchase truck payment, truck physical-damage insurance and cargo coverage require a current quote.

How do I get to orientation?

If you’re approved, VGN books and pays for your orientation travel. Ray or Carl explains arrival and departure arrangements.
